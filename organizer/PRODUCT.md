# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Plain static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS, installable as PWA. Chosen to match the existing Muse app in this repo.

## Users
Esther, mother of two kids under two, sales director at Victory Family Gebäudereinigungsservice, church volunteer (VOMI, GLS, Choir), founder and author on the side. Uses it on her phone in short gaps. German.

## Product Purpose
One calm place for calendar, to-dos and the household system from her printed "Haushaltsplaner" (meal plan with pot rotation, shopping and pantry, budget, 60 minute cleaning system). Success: she opens it, sees what matters today, and ticks things off in seconds.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Calendar (month and week), to-dos by area (Victory Family, Church with VOMI/GLS/Choir, Home), repeating items, meal plan with 4 week rotation, shopping list, pantry check, cleaning rounds, deep clean A/B, monthly and quarterly lists, food budget with purchase log. Data stays on the device, backup by file. Inputs inferred from the PDF and the chat (not a separate interview): labelled as inferred.

## Brand Commitments
Look follows the printed planner: warm ivory, gold-brown, serif titles. Direct, casual German, no fluff.

## Evidence on Hand
Haushaltsplaner.pdf (21 pages) is the source for all seed data. No real customer or lead data is invented.

## Product Principles
One main thing per screen, the rest one tap away. Calm over clever. Everything works with one thumb. Her words from the PDF stay as they are.
