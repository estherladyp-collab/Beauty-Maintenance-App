# Muse

A wardrobe and beauty planner built for an inverted triangle shape and warm undertone.
Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything saves on your device (localStorage).

- **Wardrobe**: checklist of essentials (owned vs. still to buy), add your own pieces with color and optional photo
- **Looks**: visual look board with nails, lips and hair, plus a style check for shoulder balance and warm colors
- **Today**: plan the week, log what you wore, see what beauty upkeep is due
- **Beauty**: upkeep timers (nails, lashes, brows, hair, skin) and your style guide
