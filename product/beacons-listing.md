**Description**
I used to dress anyhow and call it humble. I held on to one verse for years: "Man looks at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart." I used it as permission not to care.

Then I read my Bible again, and I learned that the verse was never the whole story. I also learned that I never hated getting dressed because of my body. I just did not know what to look for.

This planner starts with the mindset, then gives you the tools to keep it going.

- 30 day habit tracker
- 2 appointment pages with a box for your reference picture
- 2 look planner pages for Church, Work, Casual and Special occasions
- Wishlist, product list and notes
