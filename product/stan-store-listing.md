# Stan Store listing

**Title**
The Care & Habit Planner: Look Good for Your Purpose

**Short line (under the title)**
A printable planner to plan your looks, keep your beauty routine and see your week at a glance.

**Description**
I used to dress anyhow and call it humble. Then I realized that taking care of yourself on purpose is part of showing up for the people you are sent to. The problem was never that I did not care. It was that I lost track of the appointments, the outfits and the routine I swore I would keep.

So I built this planner to keep it all in one calm place.

**What is inside (19 pages)**
- Welcome and how to use it
- Your daily habits: 6 habits pre-filled (skincare morning and night, hair, nails, body oil, scent) plus 4 of your own
- Your care rhythm: when to redo nails, lashes, brows, wash day, trim, face mask and more
- Monthly calendar with appointment list
- 4 weekly pages with a daily tick-off grid
- Week in review page with a streak rule: 3 active days is a streak week, 5 is a glow week
- 30 day habit tracker
- 2 appointment pages with a box for your reference picture
- 2 look planner pages for Church, Work, Casual and Special occasions, with hair, nails, lips and scent
- Wishlist, product list and notes

**Format**
Printable PDF, A4. Works on paper or on a tablet in apps like GoodNotes or Notability. Instant digital download, no physical item is shipped.
